Awake only while they work

It keeps the Mac awake only while an agent is working. When the last agent stops, normal sleep comes back. The screen still turns off and locks.

When an agent needs you, the Mac stays awake 15 more minutes, then sleeps.

A quiet agent that is still running a build keeps the Mac awake. One whose process exited is let go.

Works with the agents you use

Connect each one once in Settings. Connecting adds the hooks next to yours and keeps a backup. Disconnect removes exactly what it added.

Hooks stay out of the way

The hooks report to the app without approving or denying tools. They normally return at once. If the app is not running, they do nothing.

The one time they wait is when the Mac is hot or almost out of memory. The agent pauses before its next tool call, up to ten minutes per call, then goes on. Antigravity, Cursor, Windsurf and Amp have no hook that can wait, so they are not held.

Sleep safeguards with the lid closed

Lid-closed mode is off by default. You approve its helper once, in System Settings → Login Items.

It gives sleep back to macOS at 10% battery, when the thermal state is serious, when the battery temperature exceeds 41 °C, or on drain faster than 20% an hour. If the lid is closed when it gives up and macOS would normally sleep (no external display in use), the Mac sleeps. Lid-closed mode then stays off until the lid opens.

The 41 °C limit comes from two days of the author’s log on a MacBook Pro M4 Pro: 33–38 °C at a desk, 42–43.5 °C after half an hour working in a bag.

Four stop rules

Lid-closed mode gives sleep back to macOS when any of these holds.

Pings only when you are away

Pings go to your phone only while you are away from the Mac: idle, locked or lid closed. At the Mac you get a Mac notification.

Send them to ntfy, Bark, Pushover, Telegram, Discord or Slack. By default a ping names only the agent and the project folder.
